About Meteor Swerve

Meteor Swerve is a free arcade game for phones and computers. You steer a small ship through meteor storms, collect coins, outlast six bosses and race other pilots online, right in your browser.

Who makes it

Meteor Swerve is made by xfroo, an independent developer. It began on 27 October 2026 as a small endless dodging game called Meteor Dodge. The same day it was renamed Meteor Swerve and grew into a campaign of six worlds, and it has been updated many times since with combos, online races, a skill tree, a daily challenge and translations into six languages. The updates page lists every version.

The aim has stayed the same throughout: a game you can pick up for one minute on a phone, with enough depth to keep you coming back, that is free for everyone and asks nothing of you. There are no accounts, no chat, no purchases and no personal details to give.

How it is made

  • Plain web technology. The game is written in JavaScript and drawn on an HTML5 canvas, without a game engine. It loads quickly and runs in any modern browser.
  • Drawn in code. The ships, meteors, bosses, explosions and backgrounds are all drawn by the game itself rather than loaded as pictures, which keeps them sharp on every screen.
  • Music made live. The music and sound effects are generated as you play by your browser's audio system, so there are no sound files to download.
  • Direct connections for races. Online races connect players' devices directly with the open-source PeerJS library. When a network won't allow a direct connection, a relay service passes the race along.
  • Six languages. English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Hebrew, with right-to-left layout for Hebrew.

Where to play

The game is at xfroo.github.io/play.html. You can add it to your phone's home screen to open it like an app; the FAQ explains how. If you're new, start with the how-to-play guide.

Contact

Found a bug, have an idea for the game, or have a question about this site? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page: github.com/xfroo/xfroo.github.io/issues. A free GitHub account is needed. For bugs, please say which device and browser you use, what you were doing and what happened.

Questions about privacy are answered in the privacy policy.
