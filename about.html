Meteor Swerve

A free arcade game for your phone or computer. Steer a small ship through meteor storms, grab coins, and take on a boss at the end of every world. No download, no sign-up.

Play now

How to play

  • Phone or tablet: drag anywhere on the screen to steer.
  • Computer: move the mouse, or use the arrow keys or A and D. Space or Enter plays, P pauses.
  • Dodge the rocks. Passing close to one is a close call, and close calls in a row build a combo that pays up to ten times the coins.
  • Your ship can take a few hits, depending on its hull. Lose your last hull cell and the run is over.

Six worlds, six bosses

The campaign has 6 worlds of 8 sectors each. Every world adds a new danger, and its last sector is a boss fight against the clock.

Each sector has three stars to earn: clear it, collect enough of its coins, and get through without a hit. Stars open later worlds and pay for skills.

More ways to play

  • Endless: one long run that keeps speeding up. How far can you get?
  • Online races: 2 to 4 pilots fly the same storm, and the last ship flying wins. Close calls charge an attack that drops red-hot rocks on your rivals. Use quick match, make a private room and share its code, or race the computer pilots.
  • Daily challenge: one storm a day, the same for every player. Fly it as often as you like, beat your best time, and share it with friends.
  • Missions: three small goals every day that pay coins. Finish all three for a free star guard.
  • Winter event: in December, catch snowflakes in your runs to unlock the Frost trail.

Power-ups

  • Shield blocks hits for a while.
  • Magnet pulls in nearby coins.
  • Slow-mo slows the dangers down, while your progress keeps full speed.
  • Blast clears every danger on the screen.

Hangar and skills

  • Seven ships, each with its own hull strength and perk, from the Comet you start with to the secret Starlight (collect 120 stars).
  • Engine trails, upgrades for your hull, magnet, power-ups and starting shield, and star guards that protect a no-hit star.
  • A star skill tree: spend the stars you earn on lasting bonuses, and reset it for free any time.
  • A daily bonus that grows over seven days.

Languages

The game speaks English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch and עברית. It picks your browser's language, and you can change it at the bottom of the menu.

Your progress

Progress is saved in your browser, so there's no account to make. To play on another device or browser, open the backup button on the menu, copy your backup code, and restore it there.
