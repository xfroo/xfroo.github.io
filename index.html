What is Meteor Swerve?

Meteor Swerve is a fast arcade game about one simple skill: not getting hit. Your ship flies up through a storm of falling rocks, and you slide it left and right to slip through the gaps. You can learn it in a few seconds, but it takes a long time to master, because the storm keeps changing. Comets streak in at an angle, blocks of ice burst into shards, lasers light up whole lanes, black holes tug your ship off course and alien saucers shoot at you.

The danger is also where the reward is. Fly close past a meteor without touching it and you earn a bonus coin. String those close calls together and your combo climbs, multiplying the coins up to ten times. Players who play it safe clear sectors; players who swerve close get rich and fill the hangar with faster ships, brighter engine trails and upgrades.

A single run takes from half a minute to a few minutes, so Meteor Swerve works well for a quick break. When you want more, there's a 48-sector campaign with six boss fights, an endless mode that never stops speeding up, online races against other players and a fresh daily challenge.

How to play in 30 seconds

  • Steer: on a phone or tablet, drag anywhere on the screen. The ship copies your finger's sideways movement, so your thumb never has to cover it. On a computer, move the mouse or use ← → or A D.
  • Survive: every hit costs one cell of your ship's hull. Lose the last one and the run is over. Stay alive until a sector's timer runs out to clear it.
  • Swerve close: passing near a meteor is a close call. It pays a bonus coin, and close calls in quick succession build a combo.
  • Grab power-ups: a Shield blocks a hit, a Magnet pulls coins to you, Slow-mo slows the storm and a Blast wipes the screen clean.

Questions players ask

Is Meteor Swerve really free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play, including the campaign, endless mode, online races and the daily challenge. Everything in the hangar is bought with coins you earn by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved in your browser. A backup code on the menu lets you carry it to another phone or computer.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It runs in Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and other modern browsers, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.
