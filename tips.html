30 tips for Meteor Swerve

Whether you keep crashing in the first world or you are hunting the last few stars, these tips will help. They are grouped by topic, so jump to the part you need.

  2. Close calls and coins

Dodging

  1. Make small movements. On a touch screen the ship copies your finger's movement and goes a little further than your finger does. Short, gentle strokes are far more accurate than big swipes, and you'll overshoot much less.
  2. Pick your gap early. Look at the rocks near the top of the screen, not the ones right above your ship. By the time a rock is close, it's often too late to reach the gap beside it.
  3. Don't park in one spot. After the first few seconds of a sector, about one rock in five is aimed at wherever your ship is. Staying still is the easiest way to get hit.
  4. Judge rocks by their size. Big boulders fall slowly and give you time; small pebbles fall fast and need an early move. A rock that drifts sideways will be somewhere else by the time it reaches you.
  5. Read the warnings. A red marker at the top edge means a comet is about to come in at that spot, and a glowing lane means a laser is about to fire. Both give you a moment to get out of the way, and the Early Warning skill makes that moment longer.
  6. Use the blink after a hit. After a hit your ship can't be hurt for about a second and a half. Use that time to get to a safe spot instead of freezing.
  7. Never wait under ice. Splitting ice cracks into three shards, and one of them falls straight down.
  8. Steer wide of black holes. Their pull drags you off course, and touching the core is a hit. When one is beside you, aim a little away from it.
  9. Keep moving when UFOs are around. Their shots are aimed at where you are when they fire. Slide steadily in one direction and the shots land behind you.
  10. Learn each boss's attack order. Every boss repeats the same few attacks in the same order. Once you know what is coming next, you can be in the right place before it starts. The worlds guide describes them all.

Close calls and coins

  1. Practise close calls in the first world. The Asteroid Belt has nothing but rocks, which makes it the safest place to learn how close you can fly.
  2. Trust the hitbox. Your ship's hitbox is a little smaller than the ship itself, so a rock that seems to brush a wingtip usually counts as a close call, not a hit.
  3. Keep the chain alive. A combo only lasts two and a half seconds between close calls. In a busy patch, going from one rock straight to the next keeps the multiplier climbing towards ×10.
  4. Don't chase a combo into a wall. One hit resets the combo and costs a hull cell. When the screen gets crowded, let the chain go and survive.
  5. Follow the coin trails. Coins often come in lines and gentle curves. Line up with the start of a trail and you will collect the whole thing with small adjustments.
  6. The coin star needs 60%. You don't have to collect every coin. Check the side panel on a wide screen to see your share as you fly, and use a magnet if you keep falling just short.
  7. Build a coin machine. For maximum coins, combine the Goldwing (double coins) with the Daredevil and Combo Master skills, and replay a sector you know by heart.

Power-ups

  1. Shields protect your stars. A hit blocked by a shield doesn't cost hull and doesn't cost the no-hit star. If a sector's no-hit star is giving you trouble, grab every shield you see.
  2. Time your Blast. A Blast fires the moment you touch it. When the screen is calm, you can let it fall a little and pick it up once things get crowded, but don't let it slip past the bottom of the screen.
  3. Slow-mo is free progress. It slows the storm but not the sector timer, so the seconds under slow-mo are the easiest seconds of the sector.
  4. Magnet plus trail equals jackpot. When a magnet is active, put your ship next to a coin trail rather than under it, and let the magnet do the work while you dodge.

Stars and progress

  1. Go for one star at a time. Trying to clear a sector, collect 60% of its coins and avoid every hit all at once is hard. Clear it first, then come back for the others. Stars you have won stay won.
  2. Stuck at a star gate? Replay early sectors. The first worlds are the easiest places to pick up missing coin and no-hit stars, and every star is also a skill point.
  3. Buy the Ember first, then Armor plating. The starter ship has one hull cell. Spending your first 900 coins on the Ember (400) and the first level of Armor plating (500) gives you three, and that changes everything.
  4. Bring star guards to hard sectors. A guard blocks the first hit in a sector whose no-hit star you still need, and it isn't used anywhere else. The daily bonus and the daily missions hand them out for free.
  5. Reset your skills freely. Resetting the skill tree costs nothing. Use survival skills for a boss, then switch to coin skills for farming.
  6. Back up your progress. Your save lives in your browser. Tap the save icon on the menu now and then, copy your backup code and keep it in your notes. It can rescue months of progress.

Races and the daily challenge

  1. Charge your attack early. The first half minute of a race is the calmest. Close calls then fill your meter, and the red rocks it sends land on rivals just as their storm gets busy.
  2. Race the bots for missions. A race against the bots counts the same as an online race for the “Finish races” and “Win a race” missions, and it never needs a connection to another player.
  3. Pause the daily challenge if you need to. The daily storm waits for you, so a phone call doesn't cost you your best run. Because the storm is the same every time, each attempt also teaches you what's coming.
