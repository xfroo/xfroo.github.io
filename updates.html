Updates

Meteor Swerve started life as a small dodging game called Meteor Dodge and has grown with frequent updates since. Here is every version, newest first. The game shows its version number at the bottom of the main menu, and when a new one is out, a button on the menu updates it with one tap without touching your progress.

Version 3.5: the website

27 November 2026

  • Meteor Swerve has its own website, with a guide for every part of the game: how to play, the worlds and bosses, ships, upgrades and skills, online races, the daily challenge, plus tips and an FAQ.
  • The game itself now lives at xfroo.github.io/play.html. Old invite links, daily challenge links and home-screen apps still open the game directly.
  • The About link on the game's menu now leads to the website.

Version 3.4: French and German

22 November 2026

  • The game is now available in French and German as well.
  • The hangar fits on small phones and on phones held sideways: upgrade names and descriptions take less room.

Version 3.3: Spanish, Portuguese and Hebrew

22 November 2026

  • Every screen, button and message is translated into Spanish, Portuguese and Hebrew. Hebrew reads right to left.
  • The game picks your browser's language, and a new language button at the bottom of the menu switches it.

Version 3.2: groundwork

22 November 2026

  • Behind-the-scenes work to support optional reward ads on the website later. Nothing changes in play.

Version 3.1: daily challenge and winter event

22 November 2026

  • New daily challenge: one storm a day, the same for every player. Fly it as often as you like, beat your best time and share it with a link that opens the same storm.
  • New winter event every December: catch 100 snowflakes in your runs to unlock the Frost engine trail.

Version 3.0: side panels and missions

22 November 2026

  • On wide screens, panels beside the play area now show your progress, the sector's stars, your combo, hull, power-ups and, in races, your rivals.
  • New daily missions: three goals a day that pay coins, with a free star guard for finishing all three.

Versions 2.9 to 2.9.2: online play for everyone

20 and 21 November 2026

  • Online races now connect players on different kinds of networks, such as a home Wi-Fi and a phone on mobile data, through a relay server when a direct connection isn't possible.
  • Races are protected against tampered copies of the game and against players flooding a room with messages.
  • Rivals move more smoothly on slow connections.
  • The reward ads on the website were removed for the time being (2.9.1).
  • New About and Privacy pages, linked from the menu (2.9.2).

Version 2.7: the star skill tree

20 November 2026

  • New skill tree: every star you earn is a point to spend on 21 skills across five branches. Resetting it is free.
  • Progress backup: copy a backup code and restore it on any phone or browser.
  • The version number is shown on the menu, and a one-tap button installs new versions.

Version 2.5: online races

8 November 2026

  • New online races for two to four pilots: quick match, private rooms with a four-letter code and invite links, and races against computer pilots.
  • Close calls charge an attack meter that drops red-hot rocks on your rivals.

Versions 2.3 and 2.4: combos and music

6 and 7 November 2026

  • Close calls in a row now build a combo worth up to ten times the coins.
  • New Blast power-up that clears the whole screen.
  • Each world has its own ambience: drifting dust, comet streaks, snow, storm flashes, a swirling vortex and alien scanner beams.
  • Music, and a daily bonus that grows over seven days in a row.
  • Coin rewards grow with your progress.

Versions 2.1 and 2.2: star guards and trails

6 November 2026

  • New star guards that protect the no-hit star, and engine trails to buy or earn.
  • Fixed the graphics going blank or smeared after some phones reset their graphics.

Version 2.0: the campaign

27 October 2026

  • Meteor Dodge became Meteor Swerve.
  • A full campaign: six worlds of eight sectors, each world with its own danger and a boss at the end.
  • Three stars to win in every sector, ships with different hull strength and perks, and hangar upgrades.

Version 1: Meteor Dodge

27 October 2026

  • The first version: an endless dodging game with coins and a ship shop, playable on phones and computers and installable on the home screen.
