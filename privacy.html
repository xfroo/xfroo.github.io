Privacy policy

Meteor Swerve · xfroo.github.io · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: there are no accounts, no chat and nothing to sign up for. Your progress stays in your browser. We count visits without cookies. Google may use cookies to show ads. In online races, the other players' devices connect to yours.

Your game progress

  • Your coins, stars, ships, skills, settings and pilot name are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. The game has no server and no accounts.
  • Backup codes are made on your device. A backup code goes only where you copy or paste it.
  • To delete your progress, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Visit counts

We use GoatCounter to count visits and a few game events, such as starting an online race. GoatCounter keeps only totals: which pages were visited, the website a visitor came from, browser and system type, screen width, language and country. It doesn't store IP addresses, doesn't put anything in your browser (no cookies), and doesn't track you across websites.

Ads

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners show you ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Ad companies may also collect information such as your IP address and browser type when an ad is shown.

  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center.
  • You can opt out of other companies' advertising cookies at aboutads.info or, in Europe, at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Google explains how it uses information from sites that show its ads at policies.google.com.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), you're asked for your consent before ads use cookies.

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward, or between levels. Ads are never shown during play.

Online races

  • Online races connect players' devices directly to each other, so the other players' browsers learn your device's internet (IP) address. That's how direct connections work. An IP address shows a rough location, not who you are.
  • A free matching service (PeerJS) helps players find each other. When two devices can't connect directly, a relay service (Metered) passes the race data along.
  • The other players see your pilot name, your ship and trail, and where your ship flies during the race. Pilot names are made up by the game, or are your CrazyGames username when you play there. There is no chat.
  • Races against the computer pilots stay on your device.

Playing on CrazyGames

When you play Meteor Swerve on CrazyGames, CrazyGames' own privacy policy applies to that version. There, your progress may be saved to your CrazyGames account, and ads come from CrazyGames.

Children

Meteor Swerve is made for a general audience. We don't ask anyone for their name, email address or other personal details. Parents with questions can reach us as described below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/xfroo/xfroo.github.io/issues (a free GitHub account is needed).
