Daily challenge, missions and bonus

Meteor Swerve has something new every day: a storm that every player in the world flies, three fresh missions and a bonus that grows when you keep coming back. In December there's a winter event on top.

The daily challenge

Press Daily challenge on the main menu to fly today's storm. It works like this:

  • The same storm for everyone. Every player in the world gets exactly the same rocks, coins and power-ups today, in the same order. That makes your time directly comparable with your friends' times.
  • A new storm every day. The challenge changes at midnight UTC, and each day's storm takes place in a different world's sky, taking the six worlds in turn.
  • Survive as long as you can. There's no finish line. The storm grows like an online race: comets join after about 15 seconds, splitting ice after about 35, lasers after about a minute, and then overtime keeps thickening the storm. Your time is your score.
  • Try as often as you like. Only your best time of the day counts, and the result screen shows it next to the time you just flew.
  • Equal ships. As in online races, every ship has three hull cells, and perks, upgrades and skills are switched off. A brand-new player and a veteran fly with exactly the same ship.

Unlike an online race you are flying alone, so there's no attack meter and nobody sends rocks your way. You can also pause: the clock waits for you.

Sharing your time

After a run, press Share on the result screen. On a phone this opens your share menu; on a computer it copies a message to paste anywhere, such as “I lasted 1:12 in today's Meteor Swerve storm. Can you beat it?” The message includes a link that opens today's storm directly, so your friends can try to beat you on the same storm straight away.

Daily missions

Press Missions on the main menu to see today's three goals. They work like this:

  • Missions refresh at midnight on your own clock.
  • Everyone gets the same kinds of missions on the same day, but the targets are sized to how far you have got in the campaign, so they are fair for new and experienced players alike.
  • When a mission is done, a badge appears on the Missions button. Open it and press Claim to collect the coins. The pay grows as you progress.
  • Finish all three in a day for a free star guard.

Tip: race missions are the easiest to finish quickly, because a race against the bots takes a minute or two and counts the same as an online race. Replaying an early sector you know well is the fastest way to finish the coin, close-call and power-up missions.

The daily bonus

The first time you open the game each day, the Daily bonus screen appears. Press Claim to collect. Playing on days in a row climbs a seven-day ladder, and each step pays more:

DayReward
Day 1Coins
Day 21 star guard
Day 3Twice the coins of day 1
Day 42 star guards
Day 5Three times the coins of day 1
Day 63 star guards
Day 7Six times the coins of day 1

The coin rewards grow with your progress, so the bonus stays worth having however far you are. After day 7 the ladder starts again from day 1. If you miss a day, it also starts again from day 1, so it pays to drop in for even a single run each day.

Winter event

Every December, Meteor Swerve gets a winter event. Snow falls on the menus, a snowflake button appears on the main menu, and snowflakes drift through the storm in campaign sectors and Endless runs. Fly into a snowflake to catch it.

  • Catch 100 snowflakes to unlock the Frost engine trail, a sparkling white and ice-blue trail that is never sold for coins.
  • Once you own Frost, every snowflake you catch pays 5 coins instead.
  • The snowflake button shows how many you have caught so far.
  • Races and the daily challenge stay as they are, so everyone keeps flying the same storm.

A five-minute daily routine

If you only have a few minutes a day, this order gets the most out of them:

  1. Open the game and claim the daily bonus.
  2. Look at your missions so you know what to aim for.
  3. Fly the daily challenge once or twice. It counts towards the “Play today's daily challenge” mission.
  4. Race the bots once if a race mission is on the list.
  5. Finish the rest in a campaign sector or an Endless run, and collect your free star guard.
